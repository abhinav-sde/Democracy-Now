---
title: Three presidents, one ballot day
dek: Bosnia and Herzegovina does not elect a single head of state.
---

The presidency of Bosnia and Herzegovina has three members, and the 4 October 2026 ballot fills that body rather than a single chair. One Bosniak and one Croat are elected in the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina. One Serb is elected in Republika Srpska. The terms are four years. The same day, voters elect the 42-seat House of Representatives, also for four years.

Who may stand for which of those three seats has been contested in court. This page records how the offices are filled. It is not a ruling on that contest, and it is not a score of whether the election is free and fair.

The useful comparison is with a country that elects one president, such as Brazil, or with a country that elects no president at all on election day, such as Latvia. Bosnia and Herzegovina is doing something else: a collective head of state, chosen in pieces, on the same day as its lower house.
