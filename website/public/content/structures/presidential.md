---
title: One national vote for the executive
dek: The Gambia's December election is a presidential vote. The winner becomes the government.
---

In a presidential election the voters fill the executive themselves. The Gambia's president is head of state and head of government, elected for five years. The date on file is 5 December 2026, and it is one presidential contest. The national assembly is a separate election and is not on that line.

This is a different shape from Brazil, where a president also has to clear a majority and may face a second round three weeks later. This file does not describe a Gambian second round, because the published date is a single presidential election. It is also different from Latvia or India, where the election chooses a parliament and the government is formed afterwards.

A confirmed date still is not a judgment about the campaign. Whether the election is free and fair is a separate, sourced question. The calendar only says the presidential term is up for election on that day.
