# August 2027 — four weekends

On the board. Guatemala’s second round is conditional. No winners.

## Weekend of 7 August

- **Air:** Sunday 8 August, 120 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** Kenya, Tuesday

### Cold open

Campaigning in Kenya was due to stop yesterday, forty-eight hours before a Tuesday election. On Tuesday the country elects a president, a National Assembly, and a Senate. The president needs more than half, and a quarter in at least 24 of the 47 counties. If not, there is a runoff, and we will not invent its date tonight. Governors are on the same Tuesday and they run counties. We will not name a winner on Tuesday’s night or on this show.

### On the desk

- 10 August — Kenya — president, National Assembly, Senate, and county governors
- 22 August — Guatemala — presidential second round — conditional
- 24 August — Angola — parliament

### The ballot

The threshold, with the map. Then the two chambers, as chambers, not as the presidency. Then governors, labeled county. Three layers. The lower third changes when the layer changes.

### How this government works

Why a geographic rule exists, without a speech about virtue. It stops a president being elected by a pile of votes in a few places. It also means the first-round night can be unfinished even when someone is ahead. Unfinished is a legal status. It is not a crisis the host needs to invent.

### The other chair

Guatemala. Two scripts, previewed so the 22 August show is not a surprise. Open A: nobody won an absolute majority, the decree includes 22 August, the second round is a presidency only. Open B: someone won, or the decree never confirmed the day, and 22 August is an Angola preview instead.

### Third chair

Angola. Parliament on the 24th. The presidency, if the law still derives it from the winning list, is a consequence of the parliamentary count, not a separate ballot. Read the card.

### The month ahead

September has no dated national election on the 2027 calendar. The September shows are the undated list. Warn the audience so they do not think the desk went dark.

### What we will not say

- We will not call the Kenyan president early.
- We will not call a governor the national result.
- We will not lock 22 August.

### Watch next

Sunday 15 August. Kenya’s desk. Sunday 22 August, conditional.

## Weekend of 21 August

- **Status:** conditional

### The ballot

Under Open A, Guatemala for half the show: presidency, two tickets, absolute majority already failed, this vote finishes the office. Under Open B, Angola takes the ballot block immediately.

Angola. The calendar says parliament. If the law makes the head of the winning national list the president, say that as a consequence, not as a second election the public marks on a different paper.

### How this government works

October’s pair, planted. On 24 October Argentina’s public elects a president. Switzerland’s public, the same Sunday, elects two chambers and does not elect the Federal Council. Parliament elects the Federal Council afterwards. The audience gets five minutes now and three Sundays in October.
