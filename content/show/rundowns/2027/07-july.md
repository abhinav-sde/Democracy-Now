# July 2027 — five weekends

On the board. No popular national election has a day this month unless India or an undated parliament receives one. Empty is valid.

## Weekend of 3 July

- **Air:** Sunday 4 July, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** India, president, indirect, no day

### Cold open

July is on the calendar as India’s presidential election, and July does not have a day. The public will not vote even when the day arrives. An electoral college will. This hour is that college. If a notification has set the day by this morning, we will say the day and keep the rest of the explanation.

### On the desk

- July — India — president — indirect — undated
- 10 August — Kenya — president, National Assembly, Senate
- 22 August — Guatemala — presidential second round — conditional, and preliminary unless the decree confirmed it
- 24 August — Angola — parliament

### The ballot

There is no popular ballot. Say so in the first minute.

The college: elected members of the Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha, and elected members of the state legislative assemblies. Nominated members do not vote. Votes are weighted so that Parliament as a whole and the assemblies as a whole balance, and so that the states are weighted by population. The president is head of state. The prime minister runs the government, and the prime minister is not being chosen.

### How this government works

Germany on 30 January was the same family. A convention of politicians, not a queue of citizens. India’s college is larger and weighted. The office is the same kind of office: a head of state beside a head of government who comes from parliament.

### The other chair

Kenya on 10 August, five minutes. The contrast is the point. Kenya’s public elects the president. India’s college elects the president. Both words are “president.”

### What we will not say

- We will not invent the polling day.
- We will not say Indians vote for president the way they vote for an assembly.
- We will not treat Guatemala’s August date as locked.

## Weekend of 10 July

- **Air:** Sunday 11 July, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** none, unless India’s day has been set

### Cold open

Still no popular vote today. If India’s presidential election now has a day, that day replaces this paragraph. If it does not, we stay on the mechanism: why a vote in a small state and a vote in a large state are not meant to weigh the same, and why an MP’s vote and an MLA’s vote are balanced on purpose.

### On the desk

- India — president — indirect — day only if notified
- Greece, Slovakia, Spain — parliaments or a deadline — undated
- 10 August — Kenya

### The ballot

The weighting, in plain speech. The Constitution wants the assemblies, together, to matter about as much as Parliament, and it wants larger states to matter more than smaller ones. The host does not need the algebra on air. The host needs the purpose. A citizen watching at home is not an elector this month.

### How this government works

Who is left out. Nominated members of Parliament. Nominated members of assemblies. The public. Saying the exclusions is as important as saying the college.

### The other chair

Three undated European lines, so they are not forgotten: Greece’s parliament, due if the term runs out and no day is set; Slovakia’s parliament, which is not the referendum of July 2026; Spain, no later than 22 August 2027 if an election is not called earlier. That Spanish date is a deadline. It is not a polling day. The show on 25 July says this properly.

### What we will not say

- We will not put Spain on 22 August as an election.
- We will not pretend the algebra is a result.

Sunday 18 July. The slot: ballot show if the day is now known, structure show if it is not.

## Weekend of 17 July

- **Air:** Sunday 18 July, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** India’s president, only if the day is this week

### Cold open

This Sunday is a slot we refused to fill with a guess. If the presidential election is this week, the open is the college and the day, and we still do not name a president. If the election is another week, we say which week, from the notification only. If there is still no day, we say that, and we use the hour on what a head of state does not run.

### On the desk

- India — as notified, or still undated
- 22 August — Spain’s legal latest — not a polling day
- 10 August — Kenya — now three weeks out

### The ballot

Follow the cold open. Do not keep a prepared “election day” script if the notification says something else. The producer throws the wrong script out in the afternoon.

### How this government works

The Indian president appoints the prime minister, and the prime minister is the person who commands the Lok Sabha. The appointment is not a free choice among the public. That is the useful sentence, and it is true whether or not the election is this week.

### The other chair

Kenya. Start the real preview. Tuesday 10 August. President, National Assembly, Senate. More than half the votes, and a quarter in 24 of the 47 counties, or a runoff. Governors are county officers on the same cycle. Mention them as county, not federal.

## Weekend of 24 July

- **Air:** Sunday 25 July, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** none. Undated parliaments, and one deadline.

### Cold open

Spain must hold its next general election no later than 22 August 2027 if it is not called earlier. That sentence is a deadline. It is not an announcement that Spain votes on 22 August. Greece and Slovakia are also due a parliament in this year, and they also have no day. We will talk about what a deadline is. We will not circle a Saturday for them.

### On the desk

- Spain — Congress and Senate — deadline, not a day
- Greece — parliament — undated
- Slovakia — parliament — undated
- 10 August — Kenya — confirmed
- 24 August — Angola — parliament — confirmed

### The ballot

Empty of a popular national vote, unless India’s day fell here. Say the empty week.

### How this government works

A maximum term is a lid. Dissolution can lift the lid early. The lid itself is not a polling place. The audience has seen snaps all through 2026 — Denmark, Malta, Serbia, Bulgaria’s parliament. Those had a person and a day. Spain, today, has a lid.

### The other chair

Angola on 24 August. The calendar line is the parliament. In Angola the head of the largest party’s list becomes president, if that is what the law card in the studio still says. If the card is not in the studio, say “parliament” and do not invent a separate presidential ballot.

### What we will not say

- We will not schedule Spain, Greece, or Slovakia.
- We will not call 22 August a Spanish election day.

### Watch next

Sunday 1 August. Kenya’s preview. Guatemala’s possible second round is the weekend of 21 August.

## Weekend of 31 July

- **Air:** Sunday 1 August, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** Kenya, a week from Tuesday

### Cold open

Kenya holds a general election on Tuesday 10 August. A president, a National Assembly, and a Senate. The president needs more than half the votes and at least a quarter of the votes in 24 counties, or the constitution sends the country to a runoff. Campaigns, on the timetable the commission published, end on 7 August, forty-eight hours before the vote. Himachal Pradesh and Gujarat in India are due an assembly election later this year. They still have no day.

### On the desk

- 7 August — Kenya — campaigns end
- 10 August — Kenya — general election
- 22 August — Guatemala — conditional second round
- 24 August — Angola — parliament
- Himachal Pradesh, 68 seats; Gujarat, 182 seats — undated

### The ballot

Kenya’s three offices and the geographic threshold. A national lead is not enough. The counties are part of the rule. Draw 47 counties as a test, not as a result.

### How this government works

Governors. They are county governments on the same Tuesday. They are not the president and they are not Parliament. Nigeria in February and March was the rehearsal for keeping those layers apart. Use it.

### The other chair

India’s later assemblies. Sizes yes, months no. Do not say November for Himachal or December for Gujarat unless the Commission has said it.

### What we will not say

- We will not predict Kenya’s runoff.
- We will not date Himachal or Gujarat.
