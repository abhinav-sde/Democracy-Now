# November 2027 — four weekends

On the board. Nothing this month is a confirmed polling day in the book as written. A decree can change a Sunday. The script must be thrown out if one arrives.

## Weekend of 6 November

- **Air:** Sunday 7 November, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** Poland, only if a decree has set this week

### Cold open

If Poland has called an election and the day is this week, this open is wrong and the producer replaces it with the decree: Sejm, Senate, the day, no winner from us. If Poland has not called it, then the line you may have seen — a parliamentary election by early November if not sooner — is a deadline. Today is not Polish election day. The Marshall Islands are due a parliament this month and still have no day.

### On the desk

- Poland — Sejm and Senate — deadline, unless a day is in the decree drawer
- November — Marshall Islands — parliament — undated
- Bahrain, Dominica, Grenada, Saint Kitts and Nevis, United Arab Emirates — parliaments — undated

### The ballot

The default ballot block is the deadline, explained one last time. A lid. Not a queue at a school. The Sejm and the Senate would be the offices if a day existed. They are the offices in the abstract tonight.

### How this government works

A parliamentary election, when it happens, chooses the chamber the government comes from. It does not choose a president of Poland. The president is on another cycle. Say that so a future decree is not mis-briefed as a presidential vote.

### The other chair

The small parliaments that are simply due: Dominica, Grenada, Saint Kitts and Nevis. Westminster parliaments. No days. Bahrain’s parliament, no day. The United Arab Emirates: the Federal National Council is partly appointed and partly elected by a limited college, not by the whole public. Say the chooser correctly, even without a day.

### What we will not say

- We will not host a Polish election night that nobody called.
- We will not call the Emirati vote a general election of the whole public.

### The ballot

### How this government works

Why the show is willing to air this. A viewer who only watches election nights thinks democracy is a count. A viewer of this Sunday sees that a count requires a day, and a day requires an authority, and an authority has not spoken. The structure still exists. The parliament of Lesotho, when it is elected, will be where a government comes from. That sentence does not need a date to be true, and it does not invent one.
