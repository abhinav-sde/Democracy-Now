# June 2027 — four weekends

On the board. Guatemala remains preliminary until a decree says otherwise. No winners.

## Weekend of 5 June

- **Air:** Sunday 6 June, 120 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** Mexico, Chamber of Deputies, today

### Cold open

Mexico elects 500 deputies today. Three hundred of them come from single-member districts. Two hundred come from proportional lists. The president is not a candidate. The Senate is not being renewed. State and municipal elections share the Sunday. The National Electoral Institute has talked about more than 19,000 local posts. They are one sentence on this show. The federal story is the chamber. We will not name a majority.

### On the desk

- 6 June — Mexico — Chamber of Deputies — today
- 27 June — Guatemala — general election — preliminary
- 22 August — Guatemala — second round — preliminary, conditional
- June — Mongolia — president — undated

### The ballot

How to read a Mexican chamber night without a presidential graphic. Two kinds of seat. One house. A president who remains. A Senate that remains. If the institute publishes a seat estimate during the show, the correspondent may say it is an estimate and may not turn it into a government.

### How this government works

A presidential term of six years is a bet that the chamber will change around a president who does not. The midterm is how that bet is tested. It is not how the president is replaced.

### The other chair

Guatemala in three weeks. Repeat preliminary. Repeat the offices. Repeat that August is not a date until the first round fails and the decree exists.

### Third chair

Mongolia. President, June, no day. The show on 13 June gives it a block of structure without a fake Sunday.

### The month ahead

July is India’s indirect presidency, with no day. The July shows are slots, not ballots, until a notification arrives.

### What we will not say

- We will not say Mexico elected a president.
- We will not turn local races into the lead.
- We will not confirm Guatemala by association with a real election Sunday.

### Watch next

Sunday 13 June. What a midterm chamber means, the week after. Mongolia’s missing day.

## Weekend of 26 June

- **Air:** Sunday 27 June, 120 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** Guatemala, today, at the confidence level of the decree

### Cold open

Guatemala’s general election is dated today on the tribunal’s calendar. The first words of this show are whether that calendar has become a convocation. If it has, today is a confirmed election for a president and vice president, 160 deputies, municipalities, and the Central American Parliament seats. If it has not, today is still preliminary, and we will say so before we say anything else. A second presidential Sunday, 22 August, exists only if nobody wins an absolute majority and only if that day survived into the decree. We will not name a winner.

### On the desk

- 27 June — Guatemala — the offices — confidence as of this morning
- 22 August — second round — conditional
- July — India — indirect presidency — no day
- 10 August — Kenya — general election
- 24 August — Angola — parliament

### The ballot

Offices, slowly. Then the finishing rule for the presidency only. Congress does not inherit the runoff. Municipalities do not inherit it. Say the limits.

### How this government works

What an absolute majority protects, and what it delays. It stops a plurality from finishing the presidency. It also means the country may have a president-elect weeks after it has a congress. That gap is the August show, and it may never arrive.

### The other chair

India’s July slot. No popular vote. No day. The college, in five minutes. If a day has been notified, it replaces this preview and the July rundown follows the notification.

### Third chair

Kenya. 10 August. Three offices. The 24-county rule, previewed: more than half the votes, and a quarter in at least 24 of 47 counties, or a runoff. Angola on 24 August, a parliament. Name both.

### The month ahead

July’s five weekends are the indirect presidency and the undated European parliaments whose deadlines are not polling days. August opens on Kenya.

### What we will not say

- We will not declare a president.
- We will not put 22 August on the lower third as a certainty.
- We will not describe today as confirmed if the decree is still missing.

### Watch next

Sunday 4 July. India’s college, and an empty popular calendar.
