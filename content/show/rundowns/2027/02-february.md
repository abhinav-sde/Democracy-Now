# February 2027 — four weekends

On the board. Nigeria’s dates are the commission’s. No winners.

## Weekend of 6 February

- **Air:** Sunday 7 February, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** none today. Nigeria is two weeks out. El Salvador is three.

### Cold open

Nothing votes today. On 20 February, a Saturday, Nigeria elects a president, a Senate, and a House of Representatives. On 6 March it elects governors and state assemblies. On 28 February El Salvador elects a president and a Legislative Assembly on the same Sunday. India’s large state assemblies are in the last weeks of their terms, and the Election Commission still has no schedule in our book.

### On the desk

- 20 February — Nigeria — federal offices
- 28 February — El Salvador — president and assembly
- 2 March — Micronesia — parliament
- 6 March — Nigeria — state offices
- 7 March — Estonia — parliament
- India — Goa, Manipur, Punjab, Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh — undated

### The ballot

Nigeria, the rule, again. Highest votes, plus a quarter in two thirds of the states and the Federal Capital Territory. Runoff only if that fails. Say “if.” The states are a different Saturday on purpose.

### How this government works

El Salvador’s shape is the comparison. One Sunday, both the president and the assembly. Nigeria’s shape is two Saturdays. The audience is choosing which sentence they are about to watch. They are about to watch Nigeria’s.

### The other chair

India. Seats we may say, because they are the sizes of the assemblies: Uttar Pradesh 403, Punjab 117, Uttarakhand 70, Manipur 60, Goa 40. Days we may not say. Term-expiries that reporting has cited — Manipur 13 March, Goa 14 March, Punjab 16 March, Uttarakhand 28 March, Uttar Pradesh 22 May — are the ends of terms, not polling days. Himachal Pradesh and Gujarat are later in the year, also undated.

### What we will not say

- We will not announce an Indian election schedule.
- We will not predict a Nigerian runoff.

Sunday 14 February, 120 minutes. The last Nigeria preview.

El Salvador’s general election is the Sunday after the Nigerian federal vote. Estonia’s parliament is 7 March, the day after the Nigerian state vote.

### On the desk

- 20 February — Nigeria — president, Senate, House

### The ballot

Offices, chooser, rule, and the two-Saturday design. Say what a state governor is not: not the president, not a senator. The 6 March show will need the audience to already believe that.

### How this government works

A runoff rule that uses geography, not only a national total. You can lead the country and still fail the states-and-capital test. Explain it with a blank map. Do not fill the map with a guess.

### The other chair

El Salvador. TSE date, 28 February. President and assembly together. The re-election rule is whatever the TSE notice in the studio says. If the notice is not in the studio, do not improvise it.

### Third chair

Estonia. 101 seats, four years, proportional, 5 percent. The government needs the chamber. The president is not on the ballot. Electronic voting runs the week before and can be overwritten by a paper ballot on election day until 20:00. That fact is for 7 March; plant it.

### The month ahead

Micronesia’s parliament on 2 March, one block on the Nigeria state-election show.

### What we will not say

## Weekend of 27 February

- **Air:** Sunday 28 February, 120 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** El Salvador, today

### Cold open

El Salvador holds a general election today. A president and a Legislative Assembly. The Supreme Electoral Tribunal set this Sunday. We will not improvise whether an incumbent may stand. We will read the notice if it is on the desk, and we will not name a winner either way. Nigeria’s governors and state assemblies are next Saturday. Estonia’s parliament is next Sunday.

### On the desk

- 28 February — El Salvador — president and Legislative Assembly — today
- 2 March — Micronesia — parliament
- March, no day — Somaliland — parliament — undated inside the month
- 6 March — Nigeria — states
- 7 March — Estonia — Riigikogu

### The ballot

Two offices, one Sunday. Say what a general election means in this country, today: the public is being asked for both the executive and the chamber. That was not Nigeria’s federal Saturday, which left the states out. It was not Germany’s Saturday, which left the public out.

### How this government works

The host’s question of the night: when a country votes for both, what can still be unchanged the next morning? In a presidential system the assembly can come in against the president. In a parliamentary system the assembly is the government. El Salvador’s notice tells you which you are watching. Read it. Do not guess from the neighbor.

### The other chair

Nigeria’s states. Thirty-six governors, state assemblies. Not a second presidential election. Preview the desk for 7 March.

### Third chair

Micronesia on Tuesday, a parliament. Somaliland’s parliament is listed for March with no day. Say “no day.” Do not pick one. Status of Somaliland: not decided by this show.

### The month ahead

10 April, The Gambia’s parliament, the pair to the president elected on 5 December 2026. France and Finland on 18 April.

### What we will not say

- We will not name a Salvadoran president.
- We will not invent the re-election rule.
- We will not date Somaliland.

Sunday 7 March, 120 minutes. Nigeria’s states were yesterday. Estonia votes today, on that Sunday.
