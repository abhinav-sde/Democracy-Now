# September 2027 — four weekends

On the board. The international calendar’s September section is empty. These Sundays belong to elections that are due and undated, and to the October preview.

## Weekend of 4 September

- **Air:** Sunday 5 September, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** none

### Cold open

No national election is dated in September. That is not a week we lost. It is a week the calendar left blank. On 24 October, Argentina elects a president and a congress, and Switzerland elects two chambers. Tonight we say what a blank month is for, and we put 24 October on the paper calendar.

### On the desk

- September — no dated national election
- 24 October — Argentina — president, Senate, Chamber of Deputies
- 24 October — Switzerland — National Council, Council of States
- October, no day — Oman — parliament

### The ballot

The empty sentence, said without a joke that fills it. Then Argentina’s offices. Not the threshold. The statute is an October object. If someone in the building already has the current text, it can be read next week, not guessed tonight.

### How this government works

Switzerland’s half of 24 October, in preview. National Council, 200 seats, proportional, the whole house, four years. Council of States, 46 seats, two from most cantons, the method chosen by the canton. The Federal Council of seven is elected by parliament in December, one seat at a time, and is not on the October ballot.

### The other chair

Oman, sometime in October, no day. The Consultative Assembly is not a presidency. One sentence.

### What we will not say

- We will not invent a September election to have something to open with.
- We will not quote Argentina’s old runoff rule from memory.

### Watch next

Sunday 12 September. Deadlines: Poland and Italy.

## Weekend of 11 September

- **Air:** Sunday 12 September, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** none. Two deadlines.

### Cold open

Poland’s next parliamentary election, if it is not called earlier, falls by early November 2027. Italy’s next election of the Chamber and the Senate, if the parliament is not dissolved earlier, sits in a window that ends in December 2027. Neither of those facts is a polling day. This hour is the difference between a lid and a date, taught on two large countries that viewers will otherwise see as “scheduled.”

### On the desk

- Poland — Sejm and Senate — deadline, not a day
- Italy — Chamber and Senate — deadline, not a day
- 24 October — Argentina; Switzerland — confirmed days

### The ballot

Empty. Then each deadline, with the sentence “if not called earlier.” A snap would replace the lid with a real day. Until a decree exists, the lower third says “no day.”

### How this government works

What dissolution does. It turns a maximum into a date, and the date is whatever the decree prints. The show’s job on the morning after a decree is to throw this script out and write the day. The job tonight is not to get ahead of the decree.

### The other chair

Argentina and Switzerland, five minutes, so the confirmed Sunday does not get lost behind the undated ones.

### What we will not say

- We will not schedule Poland for 7 November.
- We will not schedule Italy for 22 December.
- We will not say those dates are impossible. We will say they are not elections until someone calls them.

### Watch next

Sunday 19 September. Six undated presidential or parliamentary lines, as offices.

## Weekend of 18 September

- **Air:** Sunday 19 September, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** none. Undated offices.

### Cold open

Six elections are due this year and have no day: a president in Timor-Leste, a president in Slovenia, a parliament in Tunisia, a parliament in Montenegro, a parliament in Papua New Guinea, and a parliament in the Republic of the Congo, whose president was elected in March 2026. We can say what each office is. We cannot say when.

### On the desk

- Timor-Leste — president — undated
- Slovenia — president — undated. The National Assembly was 22 March 2026.
- Tunisia — parliament — undated
- Montenegro — parliament — undated
- Papua New Guinea — parliament — undated
- Republic of the Congo — parliament — undated
- 24 October — Argentina; Switzerland

### The ballot

Give each one office-sentence and sit down. Timor-Leste: a direct presidency. Slovenia: a presidency separate from the assembly the country already elected. Tunisia, Montenegro, Papua New Guinea, Congo: parliaments. No thresholds we are not sure of. No days.

### How this government works

Congo is the useful pair. A president last March, a parliament still undated. The audience has seen this shape in The Gambia, with dates, and in Cape Verde, with dates. Here one of the dates is missing. Missing is allowed to stay missing.

### The other chair

The October pair, last preview before October’s own Sundays take over. Argentina elects a president. Switzerland does not elect its executive. Same Sunday.

### What we will not say

- We will not distribute these six across imaginary Saturdays.
- We will not predict any of them.

### Watch next

Sunday 26 September. Into October.

## Weekend of 25 September

- **Air:** Sunday 26 September, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** none. The bridge to 24 October.

### Cold open

A week from Sunday we start a month with one confirmed election day in it: 24 October. Argentina and Switzerland. Four Sundays will point at that day, and the fourth is the day itself. Oman’s parliament is somewhere in October without a date. Tonight is the bridge, and it is mostly Switzerland, because Argentina’s rule must be read from a statute we are not going to fake.

### On the desk

- 24 October — Argentina — president, part of the Senate, Chamber of Deputies
- 24 October — Switzerland — National Council and Council of States
- October — Oman — parliament — no day

### The ballot

Switzerland, full preview. 200. 46. Cantonal methods for the Council of States, so do not describe one national system for that house. The Federal Council afterwards, in December, elected by the new parliament.

### How this government works

Argentina beside it, offices only. President, yes. Both chambers, the Senate only in the class that is up. The threshold: “we will read the statute on the first Sunday of October, from the text, or we will say we do not have the text.”

### The other chair

Oman. One sentence. No day.

### What we will not say

- We will not fill September, on the last night of it, with a retrospective of winners. We did not call any.
- We will not recite 2023’s Argentine ballot rule.

### Watch next

Sunday 3 October. The statute, if we have it, and the two systems.
