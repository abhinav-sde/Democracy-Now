# December 2026 — four weekends

On the board. Guinea-Bissau and South Sudan stay tentative. No winners. Fiji has no day.

## Weekend of 5 December

- **Air:** Sunday 6 December, 120 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** The Gambia, yesterday. Guinea-Bissau, today, tentative.

### Cold open

The Gambia elected a president yesterday. The term is five years. The National Assembly was not on the ballot. That election is 10 April 2027. Guinea-Bissau’s calendar line today is a president and the People’s National Assembly. This desk marks that line tentative. A tentative election is not a fact of the same kind as yesterday’s. We will say both sentences. We will not name a winner in either country.

### On the desk

- 5 December — The Gambia — president — confirmed — yesterday
- 6 December — Guinea-Bissau — president and assembly — tentative — the line for today
- 13 December — Haiti — president, Chamber of Deputies, Senate
- 13 December — Transnistria — president
- 22 December — South Sudan — president, assembly, Council of States — tentative

### The ballot

The Gambia, as the confirmed vote. Office, term, and the April gap. Draw April on the calendar.

Then Guinea-Bissau, carefully. August was a constitutional referendum. Today’s line, if it is held, is the offices. The host says “if it is held.” The August referendum did not elect these people.

### How this government works

A presidential election and a general election are different products. The Gambia sold the audience one office. Guinea-Bissau’s line, tentative, is both. Do not let the words “West Africa this weekend” merge them.

### The other chair

Haiti next Sunday. President, chamber, and senate, on a day published by international calendars. Published is not the same as easy, and it is not a result. Transnistria’s presidential vote the same day: one sentence, status not decided by us.

### Third chair

South Sudan on 22 December. Tentative. Earlier dates were postponed. Transitional offices: a president, a Transitional National Legislative Assembly, a Council of States. Transitional is a description from the country’s own process, not a score.

### The month ahead

26 December is the year show. It does not recap winners. It recaps designs, and it says Fiji still has no day.

### What we will not say

- We will not name a Gambian president.
- We will not drop the word tentative.
- We will not say the August referendum in Guinea-Bissau was this election.

### Watch next

Sunday 13 December. Haiti.

Transnistria also has a presidential line today. We will not decide the status of that territory. South Sudan’s tentative date is a week from Tuesday.

### On the desk

- 13 December — Haiti — president, chamber, senate — today
- 13 December — Transnistria — president — today
- 22 December — South Sudan — tentative
- Fiji — parliament — undated

### The ballot

Haiti’s three offices, named slowly. A presidency and two chambers is a general election on paper. The show’s discipline is to describe the paper and stop.

### How this government works

What “confirmed as published” means, because the audience has now heard confirmed, tentative, and undated in one month. Confirmed means a date was set by the authority we cite. It does not mean the desk thinks the election will be free. We do not have a score, and we are not going to borrow one.

### The other chair

South Sudan, next week’s whole show. Preview the word transitional. Do not preview a winner.

### What we will not say

- We will not invent a turnout or a victor.
- We will not upgrade South Sudan off tentative.
- We will not pick a day for Fiji.

### Cold open

On Tuesday, a calendar line says South Sudan will elect a president, a Transitional National Legislative Assembly, and a Council of States. The line is tentative. Earlier dates were postponed. A postponement is the fact we can say. A prediction that Tuesday will come off is not. If the vote is not held, this show still airs, and the sentence is that the date moved again, and we will say who said so.

- Fiji — undated
- 27 January 2027 — Kyrgyzstan — president

### The ballot

Explain transitional offices. They are created by a transition, they are not an ordinary full term, and this show does not declare the transition over. The three bodies are three lines, not one “the government.”

### How this government works

If Tuesday is clearly off by the time you are on air, replace the ballot block with the empty-week rule and move straight to the 2027 bridge. Do not improvise a cause.

### The other chair

The 2027 dates that are already firm enough to write on the paper calendar. Germany, 30 January, a Federal Convention, not a popular vote. Nigeria, not 16 January: the commission said 20 February and 6 March. Kyrgyzstan, 27 January, a popular presidential vote. Three designs, named, not taught. January teaches them.

### What we will not say

## Weekend of 26 December

- **Air:** Sunday 27 December, 90 minutes
- **Status:** on the board
- **Main election:** none. The year show.

### Cold open

There is no national election on this weekend. Fiji’s parliament was due in 2026 and never received a day in our book. This hour is not a list of winners. We do not have them. It is six designs the year actually put on the calendar, and the dates in 2027 that are already real.

### On the desk

No new dates this week. Read the six lessons. Then the 2027 bridge.

### The ballot

Say, in this order, and without names of victors:

1. On 8 February, a parliament, a presidential runoff, and a referendum shared a Sunday.
2. On 8 March, Colombia elected a congress and left the president for May.
3. In April, Benin elected a president, Hungary a parliament, and Peru both, with a runoff still possible.
4. On 13 September, Sweden elected a parliament that tolerates a prime minister.
5. On 4 October, Latvia elected a parliament, Bosnia a three-person presidency, and Brazil a president who needed a majority.
6. On 3 November, the United States elected a Congress and did not elect a president.

### How this government works

The meta-lesson, said once. A date is an office and a chooser. It is not a grade. An empty result file is not an empty year.

### The other chair

2027, only the dates a producer can already lock:

- 27 January — Kyrgyzstan — president
- 30 January — Germany — Federal Convention
- 20 February and 6 March — Nigeria, from INEC, not from the stale 16 January line

Undated is a true category. Italy, Poland, Spain, and the Indian assemblies due in 2027 are in it.
