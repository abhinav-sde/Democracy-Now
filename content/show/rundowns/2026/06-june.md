# June 2026 — four weekends

Archive plans. No winners.

## Weekend of 6 June

- **Air:** Sunday 7 June, 120 minutes
- **Status:** archive plan
- **Main election:** Peru, presidential second round, this Sunday

### Cold open

Peru holds the second round of its presidential election today. Congress was elected in April and is not voting again. A second round is how a presidency is finished when the first round did not finish it. Also today: parliamentary elections in Armenia, in Jersey, and in Kosovo. Ethiopia elected the House of Peoples’ Representatives on Monday. We will not name a winner in any of them.

### On the desk

- 1 June — Ethiopia — House of Peoples’ Representatives — held
- 7 June — Peru — president, second round — today
- 7 June — Armenia — parliament — today
- 7 June — Jersey — parliament — today, one sentence
- 7 June — Kosovo — parliament — today
- 14 June — Switzerland — referendums
- 21 June — Colombia — president, second round

### The ballot

Peru. Office: president. Not the congress. The first round was 12 and 13 April. Today is the finish of that office only. Say “second round” and say what is not on the paper.

### How this government works

Kosovo is the comparison. The public elects a parliament today. The president of Kosovo is elected by that parliament, and a court has ordered the parliament to do it by 6 October. Two countries, one Sunday: Peru’s public is finishing a president; Kosovo’s public is choosing the body that will later choose a president.

### The other chair

Armenia. Parliamentary. The prime minister comes from the assembly. Jersey, one sentence, a crown dependency.

### Third chair

Ethiopia’s Monday vote. Lower house. No grade, no winner.

### The month ahead

Switzerland next Sunday, issues not offices. Colombia’s presidential runoff on 21 June. New Caledonia’s parliament on 28 June.

### What we will not say

- We will not name Peru’s president.
- We will not say Peru re-elected a congress today.
- We will not say Kosovo elected a president today.
- We will not score Ethiopia.

### Watch next

Sunday 14 June. A referendum show, on purpose.

## Weekend of 13 June

- **Air:** Sunday 14 June, 90 minutes
- **Status:** archive plan
- **Main election:** Switzerland, referendums, this Sunday

### Cold open

Switzerland votes today on referendums. The Federal Council is not on the ballot. Parliament elects those seven people. A referendum Sunday can change a law and leave the government exactly where it is. That is the whole hour. Colombia’s presidential second round is next Sunday.

### On the desk

- 14 June — Switzerland — referendums — today
- 18 June — India — Rajya Sabha — indirect
- 21 June — Colombia — president, second round
- 28 June — New Caledonia — parliament

### The ballot

Pick the questions up from the week’s official list and read them as questions. Do not translate a yes into a change of minister.

### How this government works

This is the quiet lesson the year needs repeated, shorter each time. Issue votes and office votes are different tools. Switzerland uses both, on different days, with the executive kept inside parliament.

### The other chair

India’s Rajya Sabha round on Thursday. Indirect. State assemblies elect members of the upper house. The public does not. One third of a six-year chamber.

### What we will not say

- We will not say the Swiss government fell, or survived, on a referendum, unless a specific question on the official list actually does that — and even then, the Federal Council is still elected by parliament.
- We will not preview Colombia’s winner.

### Watch next

Sunday 21 June. Colombia’s second round.

## Weekend of 20 June

- **Air:** Sunday 21 June, 90 minutes
- **Status:** archive plan
- **Main election:** Colombia, president, second round, this Sunday

### Cold open

Colombia finishes the presidential election today, in a second round. The first round was 31 May. Congress was 8 March. Today does not reopen congress. The person who wins the presidency, whoever our file does not name, will govern with the congress that already exists. That is the sentence.

### On the desk

- 21 June — Colombia — president, second round — today
- 28 June — New Caledonia — parliament
- 2 July — Algeria — People’s National Assembly
- 4 July — Slovakia — referendum

### The ballot

Second round. Two candidates, a majority, a finished presidency. No names from this book. The structure is the content.

### How this government works

Cohabitation is the wrong word for Colombia; that word is French. The right word is separation: the president and the congress are elected on purpose on different days, so they can be different majorities. France will elect a president in 2027 and, on the dates we have, will not elect the assembly with the president. One sentence of preview. Do not wander to Paris.

### The other chair

New Caledonia next Sunday. A territorial parliament, called after a constitutional plan failed. Not a French presidential election.

### What we will not say

- We will not name the Colombian president.
- We will not say congress flipped today.

### Watch next

Sunday 28 June. New Caledonia.
