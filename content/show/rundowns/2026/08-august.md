# August 2026 — five weekends

Archive plans. No winners.

## Weekend of 1 August

- **Air:** Sunday 2 August, 90 minutes
- **Status:** archive plan
- **Main election:** none. Preview of 12 and 13 August.

### Cold open

The Cook Islands elect a parliament on 12 August. Zambia elects a president and a parliament on 13 August. Nothing votes today. The next two Sundays are the preview and then the desk.

### On the desk

- 12 August — Cook Islands — parliament
- 13 August — Zambia — president and parliament
- 11 August — Hungary — president — indirect, the week of the vote
- 23 August — Kazakhstan — Kurultai

### The ballot

Teach Zambia’s shape now, so election week is not the first time the audience hears it. Two offices, one season. A president who is head of state and head of government, and a parliament beside that vote. Do not invent the winning percentage if the law card is not on the desk.

### How this government works

Hungary’s president is elected by the National Assembly on 11 August. The public elected that assembly on 12 April. Indirect. One sentence tonight, because the popular votes are the lead.

### The other chair

The Cook Islands. A small parliamentary system in free association. The public elects a parliament. There is no Zambian-style presidency on that ballot.

### What we will not say

- We will not predict Zambia.
- We will not call the Cook Islands a presidential election.

### Watch next

Sunday 9 August, the last preview. Sunday 16 August, the desk.

## Weekend of 22 August

- **Air:** Sunday 23 August, 90 minutes
- **Status:** archive plan
- **Main election:** Kazakhstan, Kurultai, this Sunday

### Cold open

Kazakhstan votes today for the Kurultai. This is not the constitutional referendum of 15 March, and it is not a presidential election. The name of the body is the story. At the end of the month, Iceland holds a referendum and Guinea-Bissau holds a constitutional referendum. Those are questions, not this office.

### On the desk

- 23 August — Kazakhstan — Kurultai — today
- 29 August — Iceland — referendum
- 30 August — Guinea-Bissau — constitutional referendum
- 2 September — Estonia — president — indirect

### The ballot

Read the official description of the Kurultai from the card. Stick to it. If two English translations disagree, say “the body named Kurultai” and read the source’s own sentence.

### How this government works

A country can vote three times in one year and elect three different things. Kazakhstan has already done a referendum. Today is a body. The presidency is not today’s line.

### The other chair

The last weekend of August, previewed: two referendums, two countries, neither of them electing a parliament that day. Guinea-Bissau’s president and assembly remain 6 December, tentative.

### What we will not say

- We will not rename the Kurultai because the word is unfamiliar.
- We will not call a winner.

### Watch next

Sunday 30 August. Iceland and Guinea-Bissau.

## Weekend of 29 August

- **Air:** Sunday 30 August, 90 minutes
- **Status:** archive plan
- **Main election:** Iceland, referendum, yesterday. Guinea-Bissau, constitutional referendum, today.

### Cold open

Iceland voted yesterday on whether to continue talks about joining the European Union. That vote does not elect the Althing and it does not elect the president. Guinea-Bissau holds a constitutional referendum today. It does not elect the president, and it does not elect the People’s National Assembly. Those offices are dated 6 December, and that date is tentative.

### On the desk

- 29 August — Iceland — referendum — yesterday
- 30 August — Guinea-Bissau — constitutional referendum — today
- 2 September — Estonia — president — indirect
- 13 September — Sweden — parliament

### The ballot

Two questions. Read each from the official text. Then say the offices that are not being filled.

### How this government works

Estonia on Wednesday elects a president inside the Riigikogu, or by a college if the Riigikogu cannot. The public elects the Riigikogu on 7 March 2027, not this week. Indirect against referendum: neither one is a popular presidential election, and they are still not the same act.

### The other chair

Sweden on 13 September. The Riksdag, 349 seats, four years, a 4 percent threshold. Preview in five minutes. The full hour is next month’s second Sunday.

### What we will not say

- We will not call either referendum a change of president.
- We will not call 6 December confirmed.
- We will not say Estonia’s public elected a president this week.

### Watch next

Sunday 6 September, the Sweden preview.
