# April 2026 — four weekends

Archive plans. Indian results stay unrecorded. No other winners.

## Weekend of 4 April

- **Air:** Sunday 5 April, 120 minutes
- **Status:** archive plan
- **Main election:** India assemblies on Thursday, preview. Maldives referendum was Saturday.

### Cold open

The Maldives held a constitutional referendum yesterday on the idea of putting presidential and parliamentary elections on the same day. The referendum itself elected nobody. On Thursday, three Indian legislatures vote: Assam, Kerala, and Puducherry. They do not elect the government of India. Counting is scheduled for 4 May. The result is not something this show will invent.

### On the desk

- 4 April — Maldives — constitutional referendum — yesterday
- 9 April — Assam, 126; Kerala, 140; Puducherry, 30 — one phase
- 10 April — Djibouti — president
- 12 April — Benin — president; Hungary — parliament; Peru — president, first round, plus both chambers of congress

### The ballot

India, because Thursday is the vote this audience is most likely to mis-hear. Five assemblies this month, three of them on Thursday. Each produces a state government, or in Puducherry’s case the government of a Union territory. The Lok Sabha is not on the ballot. The prime minister of India is not a candidate.

### How this government works

A chief minister comes from the assembly, as a prime minister comes from the Lok Sabha. The Union government stays. Say Puducherry’s status out loud so it is not called a state.

### The other chair

The Maldives. A yes-or-no about the calendar of future elections is not an election of those offices.

### Third chair

Next Sunday is crowded: Benin’s president (the pair to January’s assembly), Hungary’s parliament, Peru’s general election, Djibouti’s president on Friday. Name them. Teach them next week.

### The month ahead

Tamil Nadu on 23 April, one phase, 234 seats. West Bengal on 23 and 29 April, two phases, 294 seats. Do not collapse West Bengal into one day.

### What we will not say

- We will not call Thursday a national Indian election.
- We will not predict chief ministers.
- We will not call the Maldives referendum a change of president.

### Watch next

Sunday 12 April, 120 minutes. The heaviest Sunday of the month.

## Weekend of 11 April

- **Air:** Sunday 12 April, 120 minutes
- **Status:** archive plan
- **Main election:** Benin president, Hungary parliament, Peru first round. India desk from Thursday.

### Cold open

Three countries vote today, and they do not vote for the same kind of office. Benin elects a president, three months after it elected a parliament. Hungary elects a parliament, and the prime minister will come out of that parliament. Peru begins a general election: a president, who may need a second round on 7 June, and a congress of two chambers. On Thursday, Assam, Kerala, and Puducherry voted. Counting is 4 May. No result is on this desk.

### On the desk

- 9 April — Assam, Kerala, Puducherry — held, result not recorded
- 10 April — Djibouti — president — held Friday
- 12 April — Benin — president — today
- 12 April — Hungary — parliament — today
- 12–13 April — Peru — president, first round; Chamber of Deputies; Senate
- 19 April — Bulgaria — parliament

### The ballot

Peru gets the longest block because it has the most offices. Two days. A presidency that can run to June. A congress that the calendar lists as two chambers, so do not call it a single house. The June round is not promised tonight.

### How this government works

Set the three side by side. Benin: the public elects the president directly, on a different day from the assembly. Hungary: the public elects the assembly, and the assembly produces the government. Peru: the public is asked for both at once, with a possible second presidential Sunday. That is the whole lesson. Do not add a fourth.

### The other chair

India. Seats, one phase, counting day, result not recorded. Puducherry is a Union territory. Five minutes, not fifteen. The audience heard the preview last week.

### Third chair

Djibouti’s presidency, Friday, one clean minute. Then Bulgaria on 19 April: a parliamentary snap election, with the Bulgarian presidency not until 25 October.

### The month ahead

Tamil Nadu and West Bengal. Central African Republic legislative second round on 26 April. Antigua and Barbuda on 30 April.

### What we will not say

- We will not name a president of Benin, a prime minister of Hungary, or a president of Peru.
- We will not schedule Peru’s 7 June round as a fact tonight. The calendar later lists it as the runoff that was held; this Sunday does not know that yet inside the script. Say “if the first-round rule requires it.”
- We will not give the Indian assemblies a winner.

### Watch next

Sunday 19 April. Bulgaria, and the preview of Tamil Nadu and West Bengal.

## Weekend of 18 April

- **Air:** Sunday 19 April, 90 minutes
- **Status:** archive plan
- **Main election:** Bulgaria, parliament, this Sunday

### Cold open

Bulgaria holds a snap parliamentary election today. The president of Bulgaria is not a candidate. That election is 25 October. On Thursday, Tamil Nadu votes for its assembly, 234 seats, one phase. West Bengal starts a two-phase assembly election the same day, and does not finish until 29 April. Two hundred ninety-four seats. Counting for these Indian elections was scheduled for 4 May, with the three that voted on 9 April.

### On the desk

- 19 April — Bulgaria — parliament — today
- 23 April — Tamil Nadu — assembly
- 23 April — West Bengal — assembly, first phase
- 26 April — Central African Republic — National Assembly, second round
- 29 April — West Bengal — second phase
- 30 April — Antigua and Barbuda — House of Representatives

### The ballot

Bulgaria. Parliament now, president in October. Same country lesson as Benin, reversed: the chamber is the popular vote today, the head of state is later and direct.

### How this government works

A snap parliamentary election means the previous chamber did not sit out its term. The new chamber still has to produce a government. The host does not produce it.

### The other chair

Tamil Nadu and West Bengal. Insist on the two phases. A viewer who stops watching on 23 April has not seen the end of the West Bengal election.

### What we will not say

- We will not name a Bulgarian prime minister.
- We will not call 25 October part of today’s vote.
- We will not close West Bengal on Thursday.

### Watch next

Sunday 26 April. Central African Republic second round, Tamil Nadu desk, West Bengal still open, Antigua on Thursday.

## Weekend of 25 April

- **Air:** Sunday 26 April, 120 minutes
- **Status:** archive plan
- **Main election:** Central African Republic, legislative second round, this Sunday

### Cold open

The Central African Republic holds a second round of its National Assembly election today. A second round means some seats were not finished the first time. It is not a presidential runoff unless the card says it is, and this card says National Assembly. Tamil Nadu voted on Thursday, 234 seats. West Bengal has finished only its first phase. The second phase is Wednesday. Antigua and Barbuda elects a House of Representatives on Thursday.

### On the desk

- 23 April — Tamil Nadu — held, result not recorded
- 23 April — West Bengal — first phase held, election not over
- 26 April — Central African Republic — National Assembly, second round — today
- 29 April — West Bengal — second phase
- 30 April — Antigua and Barbuda — House of Representatives
- 4 May — India — counting day, result still not to be read

### The ballot

Explain a legislative second round. Districts, not a national popularity contest. Seats that were settled in round one are not voted again. Seats that were not, are. Do not invent which is which.

### How this government works

West Bengal is the comparison about time. Two phases are one election. Wednesday is part of Thursday’s election, not a new one. The chief minister comes from the finished assembly, and the assembly is not finished today.

### The other chair

Tamil Nadu. One phase, held, result not recorded. Say the seat count. Sit down.

### Third chair

Antigua and Barbuda. Parliamentary. The prime minister comes from the house. The vote is Thursday, so this is a preview, and the 2 May show carries the desk in one sentence.

### The month ahead

Counting on 4 May is the first thing in May. It is not a victory night.

### What we will not say

- We will not name winners in Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, the Central African Republic, or Antigua.
- We will not describe today’s Central African vote as presidential.
- We will not say West Bengal has voted.

### Watch next

Sunday 3 May. Counting, Niue, and the close of the Indian month.
